# CASCADE v0.4.4 Implementation Spec

## Goal
Improve visual separation and avalanche readability without changing the v0.4 physics model.

## 1. Sky and atmospheric depth
- Shift the upper sky toward a clearly cooler blue/cyan-violet range.
- Keep a warm peach/gold band near the horizon.
- Add 4–6 large, soft cloud banks at multiple distances.
- Clouds must be emissive/translucent, non-pickable, and inexpensive.
- Keep cloud values lighter and cooler than the mountain silhouette.
- Desaturate/cool mountain vertex colors slightly so the terrain does not merge into the sky.

Acceptance criteria:
- Mountain silhouette is immediately readable against the sky.
- Clouds add depth but do not obscure aiming or avalanche visibility.

## 2. Avalanche body rendering
Keep the grid simulation as the physics source, but do not expose one visible primitive per cell.

### Hybrid visual representation
- Use horizontally oriented capsule primitives for surface flow and dense core.
- Align each capsule to the local fall direction.
- Stretch capsules downhill and overlap neighboring cells.
- Use the dense core as a brighter, narrower layer above the broader surface body.
- Keep deposits low and broad.
- Keep powder translucent and elevated.

### Persistence buffer
- Maintain visualMass, visualCore, visualDeposit, and visualPowder arrays separate from simulation arrays.
- Each render frame, update each visual value using the greater of current simulation value or a decayed previous visual value.
- Moving snow should decay slowly enough to remain visible through runout and the final camera pullback.
- Deposits should remain visible after the simulation settles.
- Continue calling updateFlow after simulation activity ends.

Acceptance criteria:
- The avalanche reads as connected downhill streaks rather than beads or dots.
- Thin runout remains visible instead of disappearing abruptly.
- Settled deposits remain visible behind the result UI.

## 3. Result presentation
- Delay result panel until the avalanche has visibly settled and the overview camera has begun.
- Reduce panel width, padding, opacity, and visual dominance.
- Position it lower on the screen where practical.
- Keep the mountain and final debris field visible around it.

Acceptance criteria:
- Result panel does not obscure most of the avalanche path.
- Player can still understand the final mountain state when the result appears.

## 4. Versioning
- Update HUD build label to v0.4.4.
- Preserve status drawer, Mountain Lab, fracture sequence, director camera, and gameplay controls.

## Test checklist
1. Hard refresh and verify build v0.4.4.
2. Aim and fire at a weak upper-slope pocket.
3. Verify the release remains visible throughout the director sequence.
4. Verify surface/core forms elongated connected streaks, not circular dots.
5. Verify deposits remain after SETTLED.
6. Verify clouds and sky remain visually distinct from the terrain.
7. Verify result panel appears after the overview begins and does not cover most of the path.
8. Verify manual camera interruption and all Mountain Lab controls still work.
